Rex Tennis — Privacy Policy 隐私政策

Last updated: August 2026 · 最近更新：2026 年 8 月

English

Rex Tennis does not collect any data. Period.

Rex Tennis is a fully offline tennis score announcer. It has no network capability: it makes no network requests, contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. Everything you enter (player names, team names, match settings) is stored only on your device and never leaves it.

The app uses the system speech synthesizer on your device to announce scores through your speaker or Bluetooth headphones. No audio is recorded and no microphone access is requested.

中文

Rex Tennis 不收集任何数据。

Rex Tennis 是一款完全离线的网球比分语音播报应用，不具备联网功能： 不发出任何网络请求，不含统计分析、广告、跟踪或任何第三方 SDK。 你输入的所有内容（选手姓名、队名、比赛设置）只保存在你自己的设备上，绝不会离开设备。

应用使用设备上的系统语音合成引擎，通过扬声器或蓝牙耳机播报比分。 不录制任何音频，也不请求麦克风权限。
